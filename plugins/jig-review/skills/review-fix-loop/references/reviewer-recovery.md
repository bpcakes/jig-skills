# Bounded Reviewer Recovery

Use only inside an authorized review-fix loop, for an ordinary review or focused closure verification. The parent owns recovery; adapters and their forwarder children still execute once per invocation. A one-pass comprehensive review does not gain additional provider invocations from this protocol.

## Eligibility

Allow at most one replacement invocation per selected reviewer per pass, including spawn failures. Record and consume that allowance before the replacement spawn. Preserve it across resumed collection of the same pass. The replacement shares the original reviewer's deadline, including cleanup and final fingerprint verification; it does not restart the clock. Explicit user limits on reviews, provider invocations, time, or cost take precedence. Record actual invocations separately from logical review passes.

A replacement is justified by a missing or malformed coverage attestation on otherwise completely captured evidence, a missing closure obligation verdict, empty terminal output, or an evidenced transient execution failure. First attempt every originally selected reviewer and account for their results. Recover only the affected reviewer, using a fresh context and the same scope, settings, exclusions, and assignment. Never retry because a completed review found defects, expressed evidence-backed uncertainty, disagreed with another reviewer, or recommended an unwanted correction.

Do not repeat deterministic failures without a supported remedy: unavailable authentication or CLI, rejected model/settings, denied permissions, or capture omissions caused by unchanged evidence limits. Do not change models, drop a reviewer, narrow scope, stage files, or add exclusions to obtain success. A scope mismatch invalidates the pass and follows `scope changed`; it is not an operational retry.

## Account, Recheck, Replace

1. Preserve every original report and failure. Confirm the affected child, adapter, and provider process group reached terminal exit and cleanup, or cancel and await that cleanup. A lost handle, empty forwarder response, elapsed deadline, or silence does not prove exit. If the process cannot be accounted for, do not launch a duplicate. A confirmed spawn failure with no process is accounted for.
2. Recheck the complete full-scope fingerprint against the pass baseline and confirm unchanged reviewer configuration. Retain unaffected completed reports only for that identical state and configuration. Capture omissions or an unverifiable fingerprint cannot be repaired by collecting another attestation. Do not edit while collecting or recovering a pass.
3. If eligible, unused, and time remains, announce the operational recovery and launch exactly one fresh replacement for that reviewer. Supply the original neutral assignment, without earlier reports or desired verdicts. For closure, use the same cumulative patch and obligations, retaining ordinary evidence checks. Give the replacement only the remaining original deadline; for external adapters, set `--timeout-ms` within that remainder, reserving time for parent collection and verification. Each new adapter captures fresh evidence and validates receipts from its own invocation; never mix page receipts across attempts.
4. Collect the replacement to terminal exit and apply all ordinary fingerprint, coverage, and verdict checks. Only a replacement that independently completes the required evidence can resolve the original operational gap. Two partial attempts do not combine into a complete review. Preserve plausible findings from the original report even if the replacement omits or contradicts them; reconcile conflicts against code under ordinary triage, never by selecting the cleaner report. Neither attempt has a second independent reviewer identity.

After collection finishes, reconcile retained findings against available code and contracts before a recovery handoff. Missing attestation or a replacement's omission is not a reason to defer safe read-only triage or claim that available severity, location, or contract evidence is missing. Keep verification separate from reviewer coverage; preserve an unresolved finding only when the evidence actually leaves a material question.

An unsuccessful replacement remains incomplete; do not start a third invocation or relabel it as another pass solely to replenish recovery. Deterministic failure with no remedy, exhausted recovery, or the original deadline leaves that reviewer's coverage incomplete. Verified safe repairs may still proceed under the loop runtime, with a genuinely changed state reviewed in its next ordinary pass. When no permitted repair or evidence recovery remains, hand back as `review incomplete`. A complete required reviewer set and verified final state are still necessary for convergence.

Report the original limitation, replacement usage and outcome, actual provider invocation count, retained findings, and terminal coverage. Recovery never consumes a repair round, grants edits, or turns focused closure verification into a comprehensive pass.
